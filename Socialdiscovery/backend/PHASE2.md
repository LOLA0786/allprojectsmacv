# Phase-2: Micro-Community Intent Hubs (Common Layer)

This phase introduces the shared foundation for community-driven, real-time intent intelligence.

## Capabilities Enabled
- Auto-created intent hubs from topics
- Hub-scoped signal aggregation
- Live WebSocket broadcasting per hub
- Separation of signal generation (/intent) from distribution (/ws)

## Why This Matters
Phase-2 transforms signals into living communities:
- Signals are no longer static responses
- Each intent lives inside a focused micro-community
- Real-time collaboration and visibility are first-class

## Architectural Role
- Builds on Phase-1 trust, ethics, and expiry
- Acts as the common layer for future features:
  - Co-creation
  - Moderation
  - Verification
  - Monetization
